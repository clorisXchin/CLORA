What kinds of projects does CLORA work on?+

Brand identities, websites, social content, marketing campaigns, creative systems and AI-assisted workflows. We shape the scope around your goals rather than forcing a fixed package.

Can CLORA help if I only need a website?+

Yes. A focused website build can include discovery, UX, visual design, development, responsiveness, technical SEO basics and handover.

How do project pricing and timelines work?+

They depend on deliverables, complexity and turnaround. Share your brief and budget range so a tailored proposal can be prepared. This demo does not display or imply fixed pricing.

Do you offer ongoing social media support?+

CLORA is presented as able to scope recurring content planning, design, production and performance reviews. Confirm actual capacity and services before publishing the live agency site.

Is the Project Fit Finder a live website audit?+

No. It is a self-assessment questionnaire with rules-based suggestions. It does not scan websites, calculate SEO scores or connect to AI analysis tools.

How do I get started?+

Use the project inquiry form or the Fit Finder. The demonstration form opens your email application unless a real form provider is configured.
