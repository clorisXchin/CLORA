A plain-language overview of how this demonstration website works. Update with legal guidance before launch.

✳

What this demo collects

By default, CLORA’s static website does not send inquiry data to a server. The contact form prepares an email in your local mail application, where you choose whether to send it. If you connect Formspree or another provider, revise this notice to explain that provider’s data handling.

Preferences and local storage

The site stores a simple cookie-notice preference in your browser’s local storage. It does not load advertising pixels or analytics scripts. The project-fit questionnaire operates in your browser and does not transmit answers to a server.

External resources

The website optionally loads the Plus Jakarta Sans font from Google Fonts. If you want a fully self-hosted deployment, replace it with an appropriately licensed local webfont or the system font stack.

Your choices

You can clear local site storage using your browser settings or reopen the cookie preference notice using the footer link. For enquiries and data access policies, replace the demo contact address before launch.

Important: This is a demo privacy notice, not a complete legal compliance document. Confirm applicable privacy requirements and replace the placeholder details before publishing.
